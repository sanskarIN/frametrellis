# Code of Conduct

FrameTrellis is built by people with different backgrounds, skills and opinions. We want the
project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
* Assume good intent; ask before concluding someone meant harm.
* Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
* Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
* Credit others' work and say when you are unsure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
* Publishing other people's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat channels and
events — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer through the contact form at
<https://sanskarin.github.io/contact/#send-a-message>. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer will review the report, may contact the people involved, and decides on an
appropriate response, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban. If the maintainer is
involved, say so in your report; it will then be handled with extra care for impartiality.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning that explains the problem.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations.
