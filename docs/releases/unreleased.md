[v1.3.0 stable release](v1.3.0.md). Version 1.3.1's scoped patch fixes and
verification requirements are in [v1.3.1.md](v1.3.1.md).

substantial marker, subtitle, keyframe and newer project-format changes on

reviewed, independently tested fixes and preserve the 1.3.0 project format.
